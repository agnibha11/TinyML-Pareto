# Jain, Kasper, Köber, Amft, Plinge, Seuß (2025). Pareto Optimal Benchmarking of AI Models on ARM Cortex Processors for Sustainable Embedded Systems

European Conference on EDGE AI Technologies and Applications (EEAI 2025), Naples · arXiv 2602.17508 (no publisher DOI
yet) · BibTeX key `jain2025pareto`

**What was read.** The full text (arXiv v1 and v2). The figures are raster images, so board labels inside them were not
readable.

## Set-up
- **Hardware:** Cortex-M0+, M4 and M7 on one "access all the pins (ATP) carrier board", 3.3 V supply.
  - Exact part numbers and clocks are not given in the text.
  - The runtime/converter is not named: ONNX → "self-contained C model library".
  - Static 8-bit quantization, plus structured pruning chosen by multi-objective optimization.
- **Energy:**
  - Instrument: a "Power Profiler Kit (PPK)". The version and maker are not stated.
  - Wiring: the PPK's Vin/Vout go to the board's MEAS pins, and a GPIO goes to the PPK's D0 for synchronization.
  - Several inferences run per active phase, and each model is benchmarked 5 times.
  - **Idle currents come from datasheets** (M4 0.30 mA, M0+ 4.20 mA, M7 1.60 mA), not from measurement.
- **Metric:** "total energy consumed per inference cycle", i.e. active inference plus the idle time until the next one.
- **Models:** ResNet on CIFAR-10, LeNet-5 on MNIST, an autoencoder on ToyADMOS, and MobileNetV1 on VWW (MLPerf Tiny
  tasks plus MNIST).

## Findings (quotes)
- "a nearlinear correlation between floating-point operations (FLOPs) and inference time" (R² ≥ 0.93).
- "random-access memory (RAM) and ROM usage were found to be poor predictors of energy consumption".
- At cycle times ≤ 0.5 s, the M7 wins because it finishes fast. At ≥ 2.5 s, "the Cortex-M4's ultra-low idle current
  becomes decisive".
- "in this idle-dominated regime, the marginal energy cost of running a slightly slower but more accurate model becomes
  negligible".
- Pareto fronts (energy vs accuracy, M4 and M7 only) are shown visually at 0.5 / 2.5 / 5 s. There is no hypervolume.

## Not in the paper
Trees, feature-extraction energy, vibration data, data leakage, hypervolume, battery-lifetime calculation.

## Use in our paper
- This is the closest methodological precedent for duty-cycle-aware frontiers. Cite it for "idle dominates at long
  intervals".
- Our battery model makes this quantitative: the crossover t* = E / P_sleep per family, in years on a CR2032.
- Our differences:
  - one board, many model families;
  - measured sleep power (the SLEEP command) as well as the datasheet bound;
  - front-end energy included.

## Key point for us: every result is **macro AUROC**, not accuracy or F1
The task is **multi-label**: one sigmoid per fault type, and healthy = all labels zero. For CWRU that is 3 labels
(inner, outer, ball), trained with binary cross-entropy.

## Split definitions (Sec. 3.1.1)
- **Segmentation-level leakage:** segments of the same signal end up in both train and test.
- **Bearing-level leakage:** the same physical bearing is in both train and test. Two variants:
  - condition-wise: different loads or speeds of that bearing go to train and test;
  - repetition-wise: different repetitions of the same configuration go to train and test.
- **Bearing-wise (their proposal):** "the physical bearings used for training and testing are mutually exclusive".

## CWRU set-up (Sec. 4.2.3)
- Data:
  - 7/14/21 mil faults at the DE and FE, loads 0–3 HP.
  - 48 kHz signals resampled to 12 kHz.
  - 1-s non-overlapping windows.
- Healthy data:
  - The healthy baseline records are **excluded**.
  - Healthy signals are instead taken from the healthy bearing on the side opposite each faulty one.
- Faulty bearings: 18. Split 12 train : 6 test, stratified by fault type and location, with a random fault size held
  out per column.
- Healthy split: the DE healthy bearing goes to train and the FE one to test, or vice versa.
- Protocol: 50 random splits × 2 healthy options = 100 evaluation runs, with double cross-validation.

## Results
Table 10, CWRU bearing-wise (macro AUROC, 100 runs):

| Model | Input | Macro AUROC |
|---|---|---|
| WDCNN | time | 63.22 ± 10.24 % |
| WDCNN | frequency | 70.90 ± 8.95 % |
| WDCNN | envelope spectrum | 74.51 ± 9.43 % |
| **Random forest** | time + envelope features | **85.06 ± 8.92 %** |
| SVM | time + envelope features | 81.06 ± 6.76 % |

Table 13, leakage experiment:
- Set-up: WDCNN only, DE faults only, no healthy data, fixed hyper-parameters. The authors say it is not directly
  comparable to Table 10.

| Split | Time | Frequency | Envelope |
|---|---|---|---|
| No leakage | 63.17 ± 10.84 | 64.34 ± 9.29 | 63.89 ± 9.26 |
| Bearing-level (condition-wise) leakage | 100.00 ± 0.00 | 99.96 ± 0.13 | 99.79 ± 0.54 |
| Segment-level leakage | 100.00 | 100.00 | 100.00 |

- "The number of unique training bearings is a decisive factor". On CWRU, RF goes from 1:2 → 2:1 bearings = 0.802 →
  0.851 AUROC.
- Shallow models win on CWRU and HUST, and WDCNN wins on UORED and PU. In their words, the choice is "highly
  dataset-dependent".
- Their literature survey (18 papers from 2025; 8 random splits, 9 condition-wise) reports results from > 94 % to 100 %.
- No embedded deployment, energy, latency or memory (keyword-checked).

## Use in our paper
- It is the strongest motivation that the **family ranking can flip** without leakage. Say "macro AUROC", and do not pair
  the 74.5/85.1 numbers with a "≈ 100 % leaky" figure: those are different experiments.
- Its healthy-data trick (the opposite-side healthy bearing) needs the FE channel. We use DE only, so our P3 splits
  normal data by load and states that as a limitation.

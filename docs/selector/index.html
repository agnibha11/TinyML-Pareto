Where the numbers come from. Accuracy is host macro-F1, mean ± std over all folds and seeds of the chosen protocol. Energy and latency are estimates from the virtual Cortex-M4 (deployable code run in QEMU, Cortex-M4 TRM cycle model, 64 MHz, nRF52840 active power) and include the feature front end; the INA219 rig replaces them in the final study. Battery life = battery energy ÷ (rate × energy per window + power between inferences).

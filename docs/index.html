Constraints
Dataset
Evaluation protocol
Board values are measured at the 5 V USB input; the chip value is the nRF52840 datasheet sleep current.
Recommendation
Accuracy vs. measured energy per window
All configurations
Accuracy: host macro-F1, mean ± sd over folds and seeds; the board reproduces the host outputs (VERIFY, bit-exact for int8). Energy: board-level dynamic energy per window, E = (P̄ − P̄idle)·t/N, mean of three repeats. Est.: pre-hardware chip-level estimate, for comparison.
